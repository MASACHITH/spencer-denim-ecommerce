02

Colour palette

Almost no colour: white and warm ivory backgrounds, near-black text, and black for every button and link. Photography brings the colour. Red is kept for sale prices and errors so it always stands out.

  • Background--color-bg#FFFFFFPage background
  • Warm ivory--color-bg-alt#F7F5F1Section bands, announcement bar
  • Surface--color-surface#FFFFFFCards, drawers, inputs
  • Text--color-text#1A1A1AHeadings and body copy
  • Muted text--color-text-muted#6B6B6BCategories, hints, original prices, form outlines
  • Border--color-border#E3E0DADividers and card lines (decorative)
  • Primary--color-primary#1A1A1AButtons, links, selected states
  • Primary hover--color-primary-hover#3D3D3DButton hover
  • On primary--color-on-primary#FFFFFFText on black buttons
  • Sale--color-sale#B3261ESale prices, SALE badge, errors
  • Success--color-success#2E7D4FIn stock, confirmations
  • Warning--color-warning#9A6700Low stock, awaiting payment (on white only)

Colour rules

  • Black is the only action colour. There is no separate brand accent colour.
  • Red appears only on sale prices, the SALE badge and error messages.
  • Warm ivory is used for whole bands of the page, never for small boxes of text with warning colour.
  • Corners are square everywhere (--radius: 0). Only radio buttons and colour swatches are round, because shoppers recognise them that way.

15

Accessibility

The store is designed to meet the WCAG 2.2 AA standard, so it works for people with low vision, keyboard users and screen-reader users. Normal text needs a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 : 1, large text and interface outlines at least 3 : 1.

Also built in

  • A clear 2px outline shows which element has keyboard focus (white on dark photography).
  • Every button, link and control is at least 44 × 44px to tap.
  • Menus and drawers announce when they are open, close with the Escape key, and return focus to the button that opened them.
  • Animations are switched off for people who set "reduce motion" on their device.
  • Prices, stock and errors always use words and icons, not colour alone.

16

For your review

Please confirm or comment on these points so we can finalise the design.

  1. Logo. Please send the final Spencer Denim logo (SVG preferred) to replace the text wordmark.
  2. Typeface. Are you happy with Inter throughout, or would you like a separate headline typeface?
  3. Round swatches. Everything is square except colour swatches and radio buttons. Keep them round?
  4. Uppercase labels. Buttons and navigation use small uppercase letter-spaced text. Is this the tone you want?
  5. Phone product cards. On phones, Quick View and Add to Bag are small icon buttons on the photo. Alternatively, shoppers could simply tap the card.
  6. WhatsApp button. Black to match the store, or WhatsApp's own green?
  7. Hero text. Headline over the photo with a soft dark fade (shown), or headline below the photo?
  8. Sample content. The free-delivery amount (Rs. 10,000), delivery fees, announcement messages and product names on this page are examples only. Please confirm your real values.
  9. Hero on very wide screens. The hero keeps a 16:9 shape up to about 1580px wide. On wider screens its height is capped at 880px so the products below stay in view; the photo is cropped, not stretched. Keep the cap, or use a strict 16:9 shape?
