About TU@SL Fashion

Fashion for Everyone — our story, our values, our promise.

Our Story

Dressing Families Across Sri Lanka

TU@SL Fashion was founded with a simple belief: every family in Sri Lanka deserves access to quality, fashionable clothing at prices that make sense.

From our humble beginnings, we have grown to serve thousands of families across the island — offering clothing for women, men, kids and baby that combines style, comfort and value.

Our Vision

To be Sri Lanka's most trusted family fashion destination — making quality clothing accessible to everyone, everywhere on the island.

Our Values

Quality, affordability, inclusivity and sustainability guide every decision we make — from the products we stock to the way we serve our customers.

Quality Promise

Every item in our collection is selected for quality, durability and value. We stand behind our products with our easy 14-day return policy.

Customer Experience

Our friendly team is available Monday to Saturday to help with sizing advice, orders, deliveries and any questions you may have.

Islandwide Delivery

We deliver to every district in Sri Lanka. Standard delivery for Rs. 450, with free delivery on orders above Rs. 10,000.

Easy Returns

Not quite right? Return unworn items with tags within 14 days for a full refund or exchange — hassle-free and straightforward.
