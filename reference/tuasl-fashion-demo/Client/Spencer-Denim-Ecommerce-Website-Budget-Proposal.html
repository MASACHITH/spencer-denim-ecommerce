1. Project Overview

TU2SL is engaged to design and build a complete fashion e-commerce website for Spencer Denim Industries Pvt Ltd (Manusha Weerawardene), a Sri Lankan family fashion retailer serving Women, Men and Kids. The site is built on the agreed "Classic Boutique" layout (Template 1), recoloured with a Denim & Co. × Natural Linen theme. Scope covers the customer-facing storefront, product listing and checkout experience, integrated payments, and a full admin panel for day-to-day store management and reporting.

2. Scope of Work

2.1 Website & Theme

  • "Classic Boutique" layout (Template 1), recoloured with a Denim & Co. × Natural Linen theme — denim-blue accents over a warm linen/neutral base.

2.2 Homepage

  • Hero video banner
  • Shop by Department — Women / Men / Kids
  • New Arrivals
  • Trending Items — links through to the matching product listing
  • Full-width campaign banner
  • Activewear campaign feature
  • Sale section
  • Floating WhatsApp contact button (site-wide, all pages)
  • Social media gallery
  • Customer benefits (delivery / returns / quality)
  • Newsletter signup

2.3 Listing / Category Pages

  • Filter sidebar (desktop) and slide-in filter drawer (mobile), with active filter chips
  • Sort by: Recommended, Newest, Best Selling, Price (Low–High / High–Low), Highest Rated, Biggest Discount
  • Responsive product grid, live product count, "no products found" state, Load More pagination
  • Product reviews & ratings

2.4 Payments & Customer Account

  • PayPal — online payment with automatic confirmation
  • Bank / Online Transfer — customer selects at checkout; admin verifies the transfer and marks the order as paid
  • Guest checkout, customer registration/login, and order history
  • Order history shows live shipping/tracking status per order, linked through to the courier's tracking page

2.5 Admin Panel

Sidebar sections: Dashboard, Orders, Products, Customers, Reports, Traffic, Inventory, Promo Codes, Settings.

  • Dashboard — KPI overview vs. previous period; Revenue & Orders trend (monthly/weekly toggle, last 12 months); Sales by Department revenue share; Order Status distribution; Traffic Sources breakdown; Payment Methods breakdown; Top Categories by Revenue; searchable Recent Orders table; Top Products table with sales trend; Low Stock Alerts; Customer Overview by district
  • Order management — including manual approval of bank-transfer payments, and order tracking numbers linked through to the courier's tracking page
  • Product listing management — add / edit / remove products
  • Category management
  • Sale / promotion management — promo codes and sale campaigns
  • Inventory management — stock levels per product/variant
  • Customer management
  • Admin login
