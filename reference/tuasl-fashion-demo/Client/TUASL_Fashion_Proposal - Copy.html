Fashion e-commerce website — build, payments, admin panel & hosting

Prepared for: Manusha Weerawardene
Date: 23 August 2026

1. Scope of Work

1.1 Website & Theme

Full e-commerce website built on the "Classic Boutique" layout (Template 1), recoloured with a Denim & Co. × Natural Linen theme — denim-blue accents over a warm linen/neutral base.

1.2 Homepage

  • Hero video banner
  • Shop by Department — Women / Men / Kids
  • New Arrivals
  • Trending Items — links through to the matching product listing
  • Full-width campaign banner
  • Activewear campaign feature
  • Sale section
  • Social media gallery
  • Customer benefits (delivery / returns / quality)
  • Newsletter signup

1.3 Listing / Category Pages

  • Filter sidebar (desktop) and slide-in filter drawer (mobile), with active filter chips
  • Sort by: Recommended, Newest, Best Selling, Price (Low–High / High–Low), Highest Rated, Biggest Discount
  • Responsive product grid
  • Live product count and "no products found" state
  • Load More pagination

1.4 Payment Methods

  • PayPal — online payment with automatic confirmation
  • Bank / Online Transfer — customer selects at checkout; admin verifies the transfer and marks the order as paid

1.5 Admin Panel

  • Dashboard — sales summary, order counts by status, low-stock alerts, recent orders
  • Reports — sales, inventory, customer
  • Inventory management
  • Product listing management
  • Category management
  • Sale / promotion management
  • Order management, including manual approval of bank-transfer payments
  • Admin login
