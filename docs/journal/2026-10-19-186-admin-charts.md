## What was built

**Chart.js 4.5.1 via ng2-charts 10.0.0** (plus its `@angular/cdk` 21.2.14 peer). Registered in the lazy chunk
of the chart pages only (`core/charts/chart.ts` calls `Chart.register` with the eleven pieces it uses), never
via `provideCharts` in the app config.

- `core/charts/chart-palette.ts` — reads the tokens with `getComputedStyle` (a canvas cannot take `var()`).
- `core/charts/chart-config.ts` — pure builders: filled/smoothed line with optional right axis, doughnut,
  bar (horizontal or vertical, first bar accent and the rest at 55%). Unit-tested without a canvas.
- `core/charts/chart.ts` (`sda-chart`) — the one wrapper: canvas `role="img"` with a one-sentence summary,
  text legend (doughnut) or text series key (line), empty-period sentence instead of an empty chart, and
  the figures table under "Show the figures as a table".

**Dashboard**, in the demo's order: Today banner; alert strip; period form (ours); Key figures (icon tile,
▲/▼, green/red with refunds reversed); Revenue and orders (2fr) beside Sales by department (1fr);
Order status / Traffic sources / Payment methods doughnuts; Top categories horizontal bars; Recent orders
(3fr) beside Top products (2fr); Low stock (3fr) beside Customer overview (2fr); Admin sections.
Grid collapses: three-col → two at < 1100 px → one at < 900 px; two-col rows → one at < 1100 px.

**Traffic**: visits and page views by day (line), channels and devices (doughnuts). **Reports**: gross revenue
bars above the sales table; stock-status doughnut above the inventory table.

## Left out or changed because the data does not support it

- Today banner: no "New customers" card (no endpoint counts new customers); "as of HH:mm" instead of
  "TODAY LIVE" (the figures do not update themselves).
- Order status subtitle is "Orders placed in these days, by where they are now", not the demo's
  "All-time distribution".
- Recent orders: no Items or District column (the order list returns neither).
- Top products: no Dept or Stock column (not in `TopProductRow`). Trend is the API's revenue vs the
  previous period, so it is shown.
- Low stock: no Dept/Category columns and no Critical/Low/Reorder badge — rows carry no department or
  category, and every row is low by definition (out-of-stock is not in this list).
- Customer overview: Total / New / Returning / Repeat rate do not exist in the API; shown instead are
  Buyers (distinct emails who paid) and Paid orders, then buyers by delivery district as bars + table.
- Customers report: no chart. "New customers by period" would mean adding up a paged list in the browser.
- Revenue and orders: the second line is **paid** orders (same basis as revenue), not orders placed; the
  Revenue/Orders toggle is gone (both lines are on one chart as in the demo).
- Alert strip "awaiting dispatch" uses the order list's whole-table `statusCounts` (Paid + Processing),
  not the dashboard's period-limited status breakdown. It links to `/orders` unfiltered — the orders page
  does not read a status from the URL.
